## 🎯 How to Make a Pizza

1. **DOUGH_STATION** (left) → Press **E**
2. Move to **PREP_TABLE** (center) → Press **E**
3. Go **SAUCE → CHEESE → TOPPINGS** (left) → Press **E**
4. Return to **PREP_TABLE** → Click dough
5. Go to **OVEN** (top) → Press **E**
6. Wait for "done" hint → Press **E**
7. Go to **COUNTER** (right) → Press **E**
8. Get points!

1. Check Output Panel for errors

1. Tell me what the error says

### **Can't move?**
1. Press ESC to release mouse
2. Try WASD again
3. Check if camera is working (mouse movement)

### **Nothing happens?**
1. Look for "waiting..." in HUD (means order)
2. Check for station labels
3. Read the hints in the HUD
